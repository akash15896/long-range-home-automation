TMC2209 V2.1 wiring for the curtain node

STEP, DIR and EN use the same pins as the firmware (PUL_PIN 2, DIR_PIN 3, EN_PIN 4). The limit switches (OPEN GP11, CLOSED GP10) and the Ra-02 (GP16–21) stay as they are.

signal supply / 3V3 ground coil A coil B

Clone V2.1 boards don't all print the pins in this order. Wire by the name printed on your board, not by position.

Setting Vref before the module goes on the board

The pot keeps its setting when the power is off, so you can set it on its own and then plug it in.

  1. Push the module into a breadboard, or use female jumper wires on its pins. Nothing else on it, no motor.
  2. Connect VIO to a 3.3 V source (Pico 3V3 OUT on USB) and its GND to that ground.
  3. Connect VM and its GND to the motor supply. Many modules feed the pot from the chip's own regulator, which runs from VM, so the reading can be 0 V without VM.
  4. Measure DC volts from the metal top of the pot to GND. Turn it slowly with a plastic or insulated screwdriver.
  5. Set about 1.4 V (≈ 1.0 A RMS). Power everything off.
  6. Plug the module in (check the orientation: EN to EN, VM to VM), fit the heatsink, and only then connect the motor and power up.

If you'll want to adjust it later, solder female header sockets on the board so the module pulls out.

Vref to motor current

For the usual 0.11 Ω sense resistors: IRMS ≈ 0.71 × Vref.

Check the resistors marked R100 or R110 next to the chip. With R100 (0.1 Ω) the current is about 0.77 × Vref, so use 1.3 V for 1.0 A.

Before the first move

Never plug or unplug the motor while VM is powered. It destroys the driver.
  1. Upload the sketch with DRIVER_STEPSTICK 1 to the Pico on COM6.
  2. Run watch and press each switch lever to check the switches still read correctly.
  3. With the motor supply on, send open so it re-homes on the OPEN switch.
  4. Then try goto 50, close and open. open should end at 40340 again.
